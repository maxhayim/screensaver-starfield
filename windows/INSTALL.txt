Starfield for Windows

1. Unzip, and keep Starfield.scr somewhere permanent (for example Documents\Starfield).
2. Right-click Starfield.scr and choose Install.
   If it won't start, right-click it, choose Properties, tick Unblock, and click OK.
   Starfield isn't code-signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only need to do this once.
3. In Screen Saver Settings, choose Starfield and click Settings to pick colors,
   the clock, and what shows under it.

More: https://github.com/maxhayim/screensaver-starfield
